## Platform-Specific Notes

### LinkedIn (primary B2B channel)
- Tone: professional, educational, results-focused
- Length: 100–200 words + 3–5 hashtags
- Hashtags: #MemphisBusiness #FacilityManagement #SchoolFacilities #AthleticFlooring #FacilityBranding #MidSouth
- Tag: client orgs, local associations (TASBO, TASPA, parks & rec associations)
- Best time: Tue–Thu, 8–10am CT

### Facebook (community + local)
- Tone: friendly, community-focused, photo-heavy
- Length: 50–100 words + 2–3 hashtags
- Tag: client orgs, local pages
- Best time: Mon/Wed/Fri, 9am–12pm CT
- Use Facebook Events for open houses / walkthrough days

### Instagram (visual proof)
- Tone: visual-first, minimal text
- Format: carousel (before/after), Reels (process videos), Stories (day-in-the-life)
- Hashtags: #MemphisTN #GymFloor #CourtStriping #WallWrap #FacilityBranding #MidSouthBusiness
- Best time: Mon/Wed/Fri, 11am–1pm CT
