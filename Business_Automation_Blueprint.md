# Lines & Logos — Business Automation Blueprint
**Intelligent, Full & Complete Automation of the Business**
**Source:** Analysis of `Business Prompts.md` (100+ Taskade-style automation prompts) + Lines & Logos deliverables

---

## Executive Summary

This blueprint maps the 100+ prompts from `Business Prompts.md` to the Lines & Logos business (Memphis-based facility branding & athletic surface company), selects the **best-fit prompts**, **customizes them with L&L-specific details**, and **adds new prompts** to fully automate every function of the business — from lead capture through project delivery, invoicing, and employee onboarding.

**Automation coverage:** Sales, Marketing, Operations, Finance, HR, Customer Experience, Compliance, Analytics

## Part 2: New Prompts I Recommend (Not in Business Prompts.md)

These are additional automations I recommend for Lines & Logos that aren't covered by the existing prompts:

### A. Seasonal Capacity & Scheduling Automation
> "Build a seasonal capacity planner. Create projects: Crews, Availability, Bookings, Conflicts. Create an agent 'Scheduler' trained on crew skills and availability. Workflows: new booking → assign optimal crew based on skills/location/availability → SMS customer arrival window → daily capacity check flags overbooked weeks. UI: calendar view, crew board, booking queue, agent chat."

### B. Grant & Funding Opportunity Tracker
> "Build a grant tracker for parks and school accounts. Create projects: Grants (LWCF, RTP, CDBG, ESSER, bonds), Opportunities, Applications, Deadlines. Create an agent 'Grant Finder' trained on grant eligibility rules. Workflows: schedule.weekly scans grant databases for new opportunities → matches to eligible accounts → creates application tasks → notifies with deadline alerts. UI: grant dashboard, opportunity board, application tracker, agent chat."

### C. Referral & Partner Management
> "Build a referral management system. Create projects: Referrals, Partners, Credits, Payouts. Create an agent 'Referral Manager' trained on program rules. Workflows: referral form → create referral record → track to signed contract → issue credit/commission → notify referrer. Schedule.monthly summarizes referral revenue and top referrers. UI: referral dashboard, partner list, credit tracker, agent chat."

### D. Quality Assurance & Photo Documentation
> "Build a QA documentation system. Create projects: Projects, Photo Logs, Checklists, Issues. Create an agent 'QA Reviewer' trained on quality standards. Workflows: project completion → photo checklist → AI reviews photos for completeness → flags missing shots → generates case study draft. Schedule.weekly compiles QA scores by crew. UI: project board, photo gallery, checklist library, agent chat."

### E. Safety & Compliance Tracking
> "Build a safety compliance tracker. Create projects: Incidents, Inspections, Training, Certifications. Create an agent 'Safety Officer' trained on OSHA rules and company safety plan. Workflows: incident form → log + severity tag → notify → schedule follow-up. Schedule.monthly compiles safety metrics and training due dates. UI: safety dashboard, incident log, training tracker, agent chat."

### F. Equipment & Asset Management
> "Build an equipment tracker. Create projects: Equipment, Maintenance, Assignments, Depreciation. Create an agent 'Asset Manager' trained on maintenance schedules. Workflows: equipment checkout → assign to crew/project → schedule.maintenance reminders → flag overdue maintenance. Schedule.monthly depreciation summary. UI: equipment inventory, maintenance calendar, assignment board, agent chat."

### G. Client Success & Renewal Management
> "Build a client success system. Create projects: Clients, Health Scores, Renewals, Touchpoints. Create an agent 'Client Success Manager' trained on account playbooks. Workflows: schedule.monthly → calculate health score (engagement, payment, NPS) → flag at-risk accounts → generate renewal outreach → notify. Branch by Health (Green/Yellow/Red) for different playbooks. UI: client dashboard, health board, renewal calendar, agent chat."

### H. Proposal & Document Generator
> "Build a proposal generator. Create projects: Templates, Quotes, Proposals, Outcomes. Create an agent 'Proposal Writer' trained on past proposals and pricing rules. Workflows: quote request → AI generates proposal from template + pricing calculator → approval → send → track to signed/lost. Schedule.weekly win/loss analysis. UI: template library, proposal pipeline, outcome tracker, agent chat."

## Part 10: Maintenance & Continuous Improvement

### Monthly Review
- Review automation performance (are workflows firing? are notifications being sent?)
- Check for broken integrations (API changes, expired tokens)
- Update AI agent training data (new case studies, new policies, new pricing)
- Review and adjust scoring models (lead scoring, AI screening)

### Quarterly Review
- Audit all automations for ROI (hours saved, revenue attributed)
- Identify new automation opportunities (what's still manual?)
- Update pricing and service packages based on win/loss data
- Review and refresh content (blog, social, newsletter)

### Annual Review
- Full business review (revenue, margin, growth, team)
- Technology stack review (are tools still the right fit?)
- Strategic planning (new markets, new services, new geographies)
- Update this blueprint with lessons learned
