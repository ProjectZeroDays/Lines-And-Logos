Privacy Policy What we collect and why

We collect the minimum needed to quote, schedule, execute, and get paid:

  • Contact & project data — name, email, phone, facility address, project details, photos you share.
  • Account data — login credentials (salted, hashed), portal activity, invoices and payment records.
  • Applicant & partner data — job applications, signed hiring paperwork, affiliate leads and payout details, retained as required by law.
  • Usage data — pages visited and referral codes, via first-party storage only (see Cookie Policy). Analytics is opt-in and off by default.
  • Crew location data (work hours only) — when a crew member clocks in through the Crew Portal with signed location consent, position is reported until clock-out, used solely to verify job-site time for payroll and dispatch, retained 90 days, then permanently deleted. Never recorded off the clock.
  • Business contact data — for prospective clients, publicly listed business information (organization, decision-maker name/role, public email, phone, website). Every outreach email includes our postal address and opt-out instructions; opt-outs are honored promptly.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not run third-party ad trackers. Data is shared only with processors required to deliver the service (payment processing, email delivery, e-signature) under confidentiality obligations. Request a copy or deletion at info@linesandlogos.team or self-serve from the customer portal (Settings → Your data).

Provider Terms For contractors & crew

Contractors engaged by Lines & Logos work under a written services agreement. Key points:

  • Onboarding. Signed e-sign packet (offer letter, W-9, I-9 verification, state new-hire forms, direct-deposit authorization, safety agreement) required before your first dispatch.
  • Safety. PPE and jobsite safety rules are mandatory. Surface-prep and installation checklists are completed and signed off per job.
  • Standards. Work is photo-documented per phase; documented quality failures may require correction at the provider's cost.
  • Work-hours location. While clocked in on an assigned job, the Crew Portal reports location under signed consent for payroll verification and dispatch only — it stops at clock-out. Revoke consent any time; revocation may limit dispatch eligibility.
  • Time & payroll. Clock in/out per shift in the Crew Portal; admin approves hours before each run; approved hours pay at your agreed rate on the scheduled payout day.
  • Insurance. Where required, providers maintain current liability coverage and list Lines & Logos as additionally insured.

Refund Policy Deposits, balances & changes

  • Deposits are refundable until materials are ordered or the crew is mobilized, whichever comes first; after that the deposit covers committed costs.
  • Custom materials (printed wraps, cut graphics, tinted coatings) are non-refundable once produced.
  • Workmanship issues: report within the warranty window stated in your contract and we will inspect and correct covered defects at no charge.
  • Appointment changes inside 3 days of a scheduled job may carry a partial refund per the written policy.
  • Method: approved refunds return to the original payment method within 10 business days.

Cookie Policy First-party only

A small set of first-party cookies and browser storage — no advertising or cross-site trackers:

  • Referral attribution — a partner's ?ref= code is stored 30 days so they get credit (functional).

Blocking cookies may break portal sign-in; the public website remains usable.

Warranty & Our Process What to expect, in writing

Workmanship warranty. Every project carries a written workmanship warranty. The exact terms are stated in your signed contract because they vary by surface system — we put the specific warranty in writing before work starts rather than quoting a number here. If something we installed fails because of workmanship, we come back and make it right: contact us within the warranty window with your invoice number.

Fixed price, no surprise markups. Your quote is fixed after the walkthrough. If unforeseen subfloor or structural repairs surface once work begins, they are photo-documented and priced in writing before any additional work proceeds — you approve every change before it costs anything.

How a job runs, start to finish:

  • Walkthrough — we visit, measure, and work around your calendar (games, graduations, summer windows).
  • Written line-item estimate — fixed pricing, valid 14 days, per-mile travel rate stated up front for work outside the 25-mile no-fee zone. No verbal quotes.
  • Scheduling & prep — a signed contract locks your dates; you get a prep checklist before we roll.
  • Execution — the assigned crew performs the work; any scope change is a signed change order first.
  • Photo-documented completion — every phase photographed; final walkthrough together; the photo record arrives with the invoice.
  • Closeout — final invoice matches the estimate plus signed change orders; warranty terms and care instructions included.

Data Retention How long we keep things — and when they vanish

  • Chats & uploaded attachments — kept 30 days so we can answer you and improve the assistant, then purged automatically.
  • Crew location (work hours) — 90 days, then permanently deleted.
  • Invoices & contracts — kept for the period required by tax and contract law; customer portal downloads available for one year from issue.
  • Backups — the Business OS database is copied at boot and daily, with the last 10 snapshots retained and restorable.
  • AI interaction notes — stored on your account until you delete them; capture stops the moment you toggle the setting off.
  • Deletion requests — email info@linesandlogos.team or use the customer portal's Settings → Your data; fulfilled within 24 hours.
