# Referral & Affiliate Program — Lines & Logos
**One-pager for clients, partners, and community**

---

## The Program in One Sentence
> Refer a school, church, YMCA, or parks department to Lines & Logos and earn **$500 credit** toward your next project when they sign a contract.

---

## How It Works

### For Clients (past & current)
1. **Refer** — Tell us about a school, church, YMCA, or parks department that needs facility branding or athletic surface work. Email, text, or fill out the referral form.
2. **We reach out** — We contact them within 48 hours, mention your name, and schedule a no-cost walkthrough.
3. **They sign** — When they sign a contract, you earn **$500 credit** toward your next Lines & Logos project (or a $400 cash-equivalent gift card if you prefer).
4. **No limit** — Refer as many as you want. Credits stack and never expire.

### For Partners (vendors, contractors, architects, consultants)
- Same structure, but as an **affiliate**: 5% of the first project's value (capped at $2,500) paid as a commission.
- Ideal for: sign shops, flooring contractors, architects, facility consultants, church/park association reps.
- We handle all fulfillment; you just make the introduction.

---

## What Counts as a Referral
- Any organization within our service radius (0–400 mi of Memphis)
- Must be a new account (not already in our pipeline)
- Must result in a signed contract
- Credit issued after deposit is received (not at signing)
