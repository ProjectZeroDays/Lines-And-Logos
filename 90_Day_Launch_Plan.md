## Critical Path / Risks
- **Summer window fills by April** — if outreach starts late, you lose the year's best revenue window
- **Unverified contacts** — confirm superintendent/ED names before sending personalized emails
- **Single-crew capacity** — don't overbook; quality > quantity in Year 1 (case studies depend on it)
- **Material lead times** — order vinyl/finishes 3–4 weeks ahead for summer jobs

## Dependencies
- Insurance + COI must be in place before any school/church walkthrough
- Vendor registration (MSCS, DeSoto) should start in Week 1 (takes 2–4 weeks)
- Photo releases must be in every contract (add to proposal template)
