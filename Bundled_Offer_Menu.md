## Bundling Rules (for quoting)
1. **Always quote at least two tiers** — e.g., Gym Refresh + Campus Branding = "Facility Package."
2. **Volume discount for multi-campus** — 10% off per additional campus in the same district/contract.
3. **Summer install premium** — June–August work carries 15% premium (demand window); off-season (Sep–May) standard rates.
4. **Annual maintenance agreement** — Offer 12-month floor-care plan (screen & recoat + striping touchup) at 8% discount vs. à la carte.
5. **Grant-friendly scope** — Parks/grant-funded accounts: itemize line items so they can be charged to specific grant codes.

## Pricing Notes
- All prices are installed, labor + materials, for the Memphis metro (0–30 mi). Add 10% for 31–60 mi, 20% for 61–120 mi, 30%+ beyond 120 mi (travel + per-diem).
- Floor refinishing (sand & refinish) is the highest-cost, highest-margin line. Vinyl wraps are lowest material cost, highest margin.
- Donor/sponsor walls are the single best upsell to capital-campaign accounts (churches, private schools, YMCAs).
