Questions partners ask

What counts as a "referred account"?
Someone who clicked your link (or used your code) within 30 days and then booked a job with us. One referral credit per account — self-referrals and duplicate accounts void commissions.
How long do commissions last?
12 months per account at the Standard tier, 24 months at Affiliate and Creator tiers — measured from their first completed job.
When and how do I get paid?
Payouts run on the 1st and 15th via ACH or the method on file, $50 minimum. Your dashboard shows pending, eligible, and paid totals live.
Can I recruit other partners?
Yes — Affiliates and Creators earn a 10% override on their sub-affiliates' commissions, automatically.
Is there anything I can't do?
No spam, no purchased lists, no misrepresenting pricing or services. The full rules are in the Acceptable Use Policy — violations forfeit unpaid commissions.
