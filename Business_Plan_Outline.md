# Business Plan Outline — Lines & Logos
**Memphis, TN Metro | Athletic Flooring + Facility Branding**

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Business:** Lines & Logos — one-stop facility branding and athletic surface company serving schools, churches, YMCAs, parks departments, and private institutions across the Memphis metro and Mid-South.
- **Wedge:** No local competitor bundles gym floor refinishing + court striping + logo painting + wall wraps + window graphics + exterior signage + donor walls + playground markings + parking lot striping under one contract. National rivals (Sports Floors Inc., Conrad, Martin, Finchum, GameDay) are floor-only or distant.
- **Target market:** 30 Tier-1 accounts identified (10 school districts, 2 YMCAs, 9 parks depts, 3 churches, 5 private schools, 2 higher-ed). 200+ account universe within 400 mi of Memphis.
- **Revenue model:** Project-based (gym refresh $8K–$25K, court rebrand $18K–$45K, campus branding $15K–$60K, full facility $40K–$120K) plus recurring annual maintenance agreements and add-ons.
- **Year-1 goal:** 15–25 closed projects, $250K–$500K revenue, 3–5 annual maintenance agreements.

## 2. Company & Services
### 2.1 Company Description
- Legal structure, ownership, location (Memphis, TN), service radius (primary 0–60 mi, secondary 60–120 mi, tertiary 120–400 mi).
- Mission: one vendor, one contract, one invoice for the entire facility branding and athletic surface program.

### 2.2 Service Catalog (see Bundled_Offer_Menu.md)
- Tier 1 Flagship: Full Facility, Court Rebrand, Campus Branding
- Tier 2 Core: Gym Refresh, Playground & Lot Touchup, Fleet Graphics
- Tier 3 Add-ons: Vinyl & Sticker Pack, Mural & Wall Art, Paint Touchup

### 2.3 Differentiators
1. Bundled one-stop scope (floor + walls + windows + signage + donor wall + lot + playground)
2. Local Memphis presence (summer install window, no 6-hour drive)
3. Grant-friendly itemized scopes (ESSER, bonds, capital campaigns, LWCF/RTP/CDBG)
4. Capital-campaign ready (donor walls + signage for churches/private schools/YMCAs)
5. MWBE positioning (if applicable)

## 3. Market Analysis
### 3.1 Target Market
- **Primary:** School districts (10 Tier-1, 200+ universe within 400 mi)
- **Secondary:** Churches, YMCAs, parks & recreation departments
- **Tertiary:** Private/independent schools, higher-ed campus recreation

### 3.2 Market Size (TAM/SAM/SOM)
- TAM: All K-12 districts, churches, YMCAs, parks depts, private schools within 400 mi of Memphis
- SAM: Accounts with gym floors + athletic programs + capital budgets (Tier 1 + Tier 2)
- SOM: 15–25 projects in Year 1 (realistic capture given sales cycle + capacity)

### 3.3 Competitive Landscape (see Competitive_Battle_Card.md)
- National giants (Robbins, Connor, Horner, Tarkett) — too expensive/distant; not day-to-day competitors
- Regional floor contractors (Sports Floors Inc., Conrad, Martin, Finchum, GameDay) — floor-only, no branding/wraps/signage/lot work
- Sign/franchise shops (FASTSIGNS, Signarama, Image360) — graphics only, no floor refinishing
- **Gap:** No local vendor offers the full bundled package

### 3.4 Market Trends
- Post-COVID facility refresh spending (ESSER/ARP funds, bond measures)
- Mascot rebrands and DEI-driven identity updates
- Pickleball court boom (schools, churches, parks)
- Donor/sponsor wall demand for capital campaigns
- Fleet rebranding as districts modernize buses

## 4. Sales & Marketing Strategy
### 4.1 Targeting (see Memphis_Tier1_Prospect_Database.xlsx)
- 30 Tier-1 accounts scored on 6-factor model (Athletics 25%, Branding 20%, Fleet 15%, Facilities 15%, Budget 15%, Distance 10%)
- Budget Capacity as gatekeeper (score < 5 = gated out)

### 4.2 Outreach (see Outreach_Sequences.md)
- Segment-specific sequences (districts, churches, YMCAs, parks, private schools)
- Timing aligned to budget cycles and campaign seasons
- Max 3 touches per cycle; re-engage next cycle

### 4.3 Sales Process
1. Identify (Tier 1 list + scoring)
2. Research (verified contact, budget signals, recent triggers)
3. Outreach (segment sequence)
4. Walkthrough (no-cost facility assessment)
5. Quote (bundled package + à la carte options)
6. Close (summer install window, phased if needed)
7. Retain (annual maintenance agreement)

### 4.4 Marketing Channels
- Direct outreach (email + phone) to decision makers
- Facility walkthroughs and photo case studies
- Referrals from completed projects (school-to-school, church-to-church)
- MWBE certification leverage for public procurement
- Local association presence (TASBO, TASSP, church associations, parks & rec associations)
- Website with project gallery + case studies

## 5. Operations Plan
### 5.1 Service Delivery
- In-house: floor refinishing, striping, logo painting, vinyl install, signage, mural painting
- Subcontract (if needed): sealcoating, large-format printing, electrical for signage
- Crew structure: 2–3 install crews, 1 design/graphics specialist, 1 project manager

### 5.2 Equipment & Materials
- Floor sanding/refinishing equipment, striping machines, vinyl printers/cutters (or outsource printing), lifts, scaffolding
- Material suppliers: wood floor finishes, paints, vinyl media, signage substrates

### 5.3 Quality & Safety
- MFMA/NWFA compliance for wood floors
- Licensed/insured, OSHA safety, background checks for school access
- Warranty: 1-year workmanship; manufacturer warranties on materials

### 5.4 Capacity
- Year 1: 15–25 projects (summer window constraint)
- Summer (May–Aug) = peak; off-season = maintenance + smaller projects + sales

## 6. Financial Plan
### 6.1 Revenue Projections (rough)
- Year 1: $250K–$500K (15–25 projects avg $15K–$20K)
- Year 2: $500K–$900K (repeat clients + maintenance agreements + referrals)
- Year 3: $900K–$1.5M (expanded crew + territory)

### 6.2 Cost Structure
- Labor (crews + PM): 35–45% of revenue
- Materials: 20–30%
- Equipment (depreciation + maintenance): 5–10%
- Overhead (insurance, vehicles, rent, admin): 15–20%
- Marketing/sales: 5–10%

### 6.3 Key Metrics
- Gross margin target: 35–45%
- Average project value: $15K–$20K (Year 1)
- Maintenance agreement attach rate: 30%+ of floor projects
- Customer acquisition cost: <$1,500 (direct outreach + referrals)
- Repeat/referral revenue: 40%+ by Year 2

### 6.4 Funding Needs
- Startup capital: equipment, vehicle, insurance, initial marketing, working capital
- Options: self-funded, SBA loan, equipment financing, or revenue-based financing
- Break-even: ~$200K–$250K annual revenue (estimated)

## 7. Team & Organization
- Founder/owner: sales, project management, client relationships
- Floor crew lead + 2–3 installers
- Graphics/signage specialist (vinyl, wraps, signage)
- Part-time: bookkeeper, marketing/SEO, admin
- Year 2+: add second crew, dedicated sales rep

## 8. Risk Analysis
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Seasonal concentration (summer only) | Off-season: maintenance, smaller projects, indoor work, sales |
| Single large account dependency | Diversify across districts/churches/parks; no account >20% of revenue |
| Competitor response (Sports Floors adds wraps) | Speed + relationships; they'd need to hire/train; you're already there |
| Labor shortage | Train apprentices; subcontract overflow; pay competitive wages |
| Material cost inflation | Fixed-price quotes with material escalation clauses; annual price review |
| Weather (outdoor lot/playground work) | Schedule outdoor work in spring/fall; indoor work in summer |

## 9. Milestones & Timeline
- **Month 1–2:** Finalize service packages + pricing; build Tier-1 prospect list (done); set up CRM; launch outreach to 30 Tier-1 accounts
- **Month 3–4:** First walkthroughs and quotes; close first 3–5 projects (likely churches/small districts)
- **Month 5–8:** Execute summer install window; photo case studies; referral asks
- **Month 9–12:** Annual maintenance agreements; expand to Tier-2 accounts; evaluate crew capacity for Year 2
