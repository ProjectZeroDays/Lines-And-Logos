Questions

Straight answers

How far will you travel for a project?
Our standard service area is a 400-mile radius around Memphis — Nashville, Little Rock, Jackson, Birmingham, St. Louis, Louisville, Atlanta, New Orleans, and everywhere between. Multi-facility or district-wide contracts can reach farther; just ask.
How much does a gym floor refinish cost?
It depends on square footage, the floor's condition, and your finish system. The instant quote calculator gives a real line-item number in about 30 seconds, with estimated tax for your location included. Written estimates are valid 14 days.
How long does the work take?
A court restripe is usually a one-day job. A full sand-and-finish typically runs about a week depending on square footage, coats, and cure time. Your estimate states exact dates — and we schedule around your events.
What warranty do I get?
Every project carries a written workmanship warranty. Exact terms are stated in your signed contract because they vary by surface system — we put the specific warranty in writing before work starts, not after.
Can I see your past work?
Every job is photo-documented from walkthrough to closeout. The gallery shows completed projects, and case studies publish when clients approve them in writing.
Do you support school procurement requirements?
Yes — purchase orders, W-9s, Certificates of Insurance naming your district as Additional Insured, and off-hours campus scheduling are all standard. Tell your facilities office we've done this before.

Ready to put your name on the floor?

Book a free walkthrough anywhere in our 400-mile service area. We measure, photograph, and send a fixed quote — typically within 48 hours, valid 14 days.
