## 5. Donor Wall — Capital Campaign Integration

If {{Client}} is running a capital campaign, the donor wall is the single highest-ROI element in this package:

- **Installed before the dedication** — names and tiers visible at the ribbon cutting
- **Tier structure** drives giving levels (Platinum $10K+, Gold $5K+, Silver $1K+, Bronze $250+)
- **QR codes** link to donor profiles, video testimonials, or campaign progress
- **Backlit/dimensional options** for top-tier donors (premium look, premium price)
- **Expandable design** — leave room for future donors; modular panels add easily

**Suggested donor wall budget:** 3–5% of total campaign goal. A $500K campaign → $15K–$25K donor wall.
