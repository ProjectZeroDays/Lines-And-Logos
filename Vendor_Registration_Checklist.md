# Vendor Registration Checklist — Lines & Logos
**For selling to school districts, churches, YMCAs, parks depts, and private schools in the Memphis metro**

> Public entities (school districts, parks departments, city/county governments) almost always require vendor registration before they can issue a PO. Register early — it takes 2–4 weeks and is a prerequisite for most bids/RFPs.

---

## Tier 1 — Register Immediately (Week 1)

### Memphis-Shelby County Schools (MSCS)
- [ ] **Procurement Services** — register as vendor: scsk12.org/procurement
- [ ] **Office of Economic Opportunity (OEO)** — MWBE registration: scsk12.org/oeo/
  - [ ] Submit MWBE certification (if applicable) — woman-owned / minority-owned
  - [ ] Upload: W-9, certificate of insurance, business license, references
- [ ] **Facilities Planning & Property** — contact for walkthrough scheduling: scsk12.org/fp/
- [ ] **Vendor portal login** saved + bookmarked
- [ ] **Insurance COI** on file with MSCS (general liability + workers' comp)
- [ ] **Notes:** MSCS uses BoardDocs for board meetings; watch for capital/bond agenda items

### DeSoto County School District (DCS)

## Churches & Private Schools (no formal vendor registration, but...)

### Churches (Bellevue Baptist, Hope Church, Second Baptist, etc.)
- [ ] Identify: Senior Pastor, Executive Director, Facilities Director, Business Administrator
- [ ] Request: facility assessment walkthrough (no-cost)
- [ ] Provide: COI, references, photo portfolio
- [ ] Note: Churches buy on stewardship campaigns and capital campaigns — time outreach to campaign seasons
- [ ] Donor wall + signage = strongest angle for capital campaigns

### Private/Independent Schools (Briarcrest, St. George's, Lausanne, ECS, Harding)
- [ ] Identify: Head of School, Director of Facilities, Athletic Director, Development Director
- [ ] Request: campus walkthrough
- [ ] Provide: COI, references, photo portfolio, case studies
- [ ] Note: Private schools have donor-funded capital projects — pitch donor wall + campus branding package

## Universal Vendor Packet (prepare once, reuse everywhere)

- [ ] W-9 (signed)
- [ ] Certificate of Insurance (GL + workers' comp) — $1M/$2M typical
- [ ] Business license (TN + MS if operating in both states)
- [ ] References (3 recent clients with contact info)
- [ ] Photo portfolio (gym floors, logos, wall wraps, donor walls, signage)
- [ ] Company overview (1 page: services, service radius, crew size, years in business)
- [ ] MWBE certification (if applicable)
- [ ] EIN / tax ID
- [ ] Banking info for ACH/direct deposit (some districts require)
- [ ] Safety plan / OSHA compliance statement (for school access)
- [ ] Background check policy (for crew members working in schools)

---

## Ongoing Maintenance
- [ ] Monitor bid/RFP portals weekly (MSCS, DeSoto, Bartlett, Collierville, CMCSS, Tupelo)
- [ ] Re-register annually (most districts require annual renewal)
- [ ] Update COI before expiration (insurance renewal)
- [ ] Track all registrations in CRM (account, portal, login, renewal date, status)
- [ ] Set calendar reminders 60 days before each renewal
