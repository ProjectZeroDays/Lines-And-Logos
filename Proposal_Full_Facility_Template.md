## 6. Terms & Conditions

1. **Deposit:** 50% due on contract signing; 50% due on completion (or Net 30 for established accounts).
2. **Install window:** Summer (May–August) preferred; off-season work available at standard rates (no premium).
3. **Change orders:** Any scope change after design approval requires written change order and may adjust price/timeline.
4. **Warranty:** 1-year workmanship warranty on all installs; manufacturer warranties on materials (vinyl, finishes, paints).
5. **Insurance:** Lines & Logos carries general liability and workers' comp; certificate of insurance provided on request.
6. **Access:** Client to provide building access, parking, and power during install. After-hours/weekend work available at 10% premium.
7. **Cancellation:** Client may cancel before material ordering for full deposit refund; after material ordering, deposit is non-refundable.
8. **Photo release:** Client grants Lines & Logos permission to photograph completed work for marketing, website, and proposals (faces blurred unless release on file).
