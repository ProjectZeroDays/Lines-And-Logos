Case studies,
documented or not at all

We publish a case study only when three things are true: the project is complete, the photos are ours, and the client has approved the write-up. No invented results, no stock photos, no borrowed testimonials — ever.

What every study documents

01 — SITUATIONFacility & surface

School district, church, or rec center; floor system, age, and what was failing.

02 — SCOPEExactly what we did

Services performed, materials and systems used, colors and layout decisions.

03 — TIMELINEReal dates

Walkthrough, start, and completion — including how we scheduled around games and events.

04 — EVIDENCEPhoto record

Before, during, and after photos from our own documentation, published with approval.

  • Client identity protection: schools and churches are named only when they approve; otherwise studies are published by type and city ("a 1,200-student district in West Tennessee").
  • Numbers are measured, not estimated: square footage, court counts, and schedules come from the signed work record, not memory.
