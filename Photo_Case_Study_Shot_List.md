# Photo Case-Study Shot List — Donor Wall + Campus Branding Package
**Lines & Logos | For sales collateral, website gallery, and outreach**

> Purpose: Build a reusable photo set that proves the "one-stop" value proposition — floor + walls + windows + signage + donor wall + lot + playground — in a single cohesive campus story. Use for website gallery, proposals, outreach attachments, and social media.

## Pre-Shoot Checklist
- [ ] Client permission + photo release signed (include in contract)
- [ ] Schedule during good weather (exterior shots) and when building is unoccupied (interior)
- [ ] Bring: tripod, wide-angle lens, detail lens, color card, measuring tape, step stool, cleaning supplies for surfaces
- [ ] Shoot **before** AND **after** for every project (before shots are the strongest sales tool)
- [ ] Capture 3 "hero" shots per project (one floor, one wall/donor wall, one exterior) for proposals

## Shot List — 30 shots per project

### A. Before (5 shots) — establishes the problem
1. Gym floor — wide, showing wear, faded lines, dull finish
2. Center-court area — close-up of faded/peeling logo
3. Wall (gym or hallway) — blank/bare or dated paint
4. Donor/sponsor area — before (blank wall or outdated plaque)
5. Exterior — faded signage or blank wall at entrance

### B. Gym Floor (6 shots)
6. Wide hero shot of finished floor (center court visible, lines crisp)
7. Center-court logo — straight-on, centered, full frame
8. Center-court logo — 45° angle showing depth/finish
9. Game lines detail — basketball key + volleyball lines, crisp edges
10. Floor finish sheen — low angle, raking light to show gloss
11. Baseline/ sideline — full-length perspective down the court

### C. Wall Wraps & Graphics (5 shots)
12. Gym wall wrap — wide, showing full mural/graphic
13. Hallway wrap — perspective shot down the corridor
14. Cafeteria/lobby wall graphic — wide
15. Detail of wrap edge/seam (proves quality install)
16. Wrap with people for scale (students/athletes in frame)

### D. Donor / Sponsor Wall (5 shots)
17. Full donor wall — wide, all tiers visible
18. Tier close-up — top-tier donor names (gold/platinum)
19. Mid-tier close-up — names + logos
20. Wall with ambient lighting — shows dimension/depth
21. Donor wall at dedication event (if timing allows) — people + wall

### E. Window Graphics (3 shots)
22. Exterior window graphic — from outside, showing branding
23. Interior view through graphic — shows light transmission
24. Detail of graphic edge/adhesion

### F. Signage & Exterior (4 shots)
25. Exterior building signage — full front elevation
26. Entrance/lobby signage — interior
27. Wayfinding signs (halls, restrooms, offices)
28. Parking lot sign + sealcoated lot

### G. Playground & Lot (2 shots)
29. Playground markings — aerial or high angle (use drone if available)
30. Parking lot striping — straight-down or 45° angle, crisp lines

## Hero Shot Priority (for proposals & website)
Use these 3 as the "cover" for each case study:
1. **Floor hero** (shot #6) — the "wow" wide shot
2. **Donor wall hero** (shot #17) — proves capital-campaign value
3. **Exterior hero** (shot #25) — proves campus-wide transformation

## Photo Specs
- **Resolution:** Minimum 4000px on long edge (for print + web)
- **Format:** RAW + JPEG; deliver edited JPEGs (sRGB, sharpened) + 2–3 RAW selects
- **Naming convention:** `ProjectName_001_Description.jpg` (e.g., `MSCS_NortheastPrep_006_FloorHero.jpg`)
- **Color:** Shoot with color card in first frame of each location; correct in post
- **Lighting:** Natural light preferred for interiors; avoid mixed color temps; use tripod for low-light gym shots
- **People:** Include 2–3 shots with students/athletes/families for scale and emotion (with signed releases)

## Video (optional, high impact)
- 60-second walkthrough: enter building → gym floor → center logo → wall wraps → donor wall → exterior signage → parking lot → playground
- 15-second social cut: floor hero + donor wall + exterior (vertical 9:16 for Reels/TikTok, horizontal 16:9 for LinkedIn/YouTube)
- Drone aerial of campus (if budget allows) — single most powerful "before/after" asset

## Storage & Organization
- Folder structure: `/CaseStudies/{ClientName}/{Date}/Before/` and `/After/`
- Keep RAWs in `/RAW/` subfolder; edited JPEGs in `/Edited/`
- Tag each project in CRM with "case study ready" once 6+ after shots are delivered
- Reuse top 3 hero shots in: website gallery, proposal cover, outreach Email 2 attachment, social post

## Consent & Usage
- Photo release in every contract (rights to use for marketing, website, proposals, social)
- Blur or omit student faces unless release on file; prefer shots with backs to camera or with signed releases
- For churches/private schools, confirm with communications director before posting
- Credit the client in captions: "Installed for {{Client}} — {{City}}, {{State}}"
